Letter Gravity Privacy Policy

Effective July 26, 2026

The short version: Letter Gravity collects no personal data whatsoever. Your game progress and scores stay on your device only. We don't track, analyze, or store anything about your gameplay.

Who we are

Letter Gravity is an independent, personal-use word game app. This policy covers the Letter Gravity iOS app and the companion website used to host game information and provide support.

What data we collect

We collect no data. Letter Gravity does not:

  • Require user accounts or logins
  • Collect any personal information
  • Track gameplay, scores, or preferences
  • Send data to our servers or any third party
  • Use analytics or tracking software
  • Include advertising or ad networks
  • Access your camera, microphone, contacts, photos, or location

Your game data

All your game progress, high scores, and settings are stored exclusively on your device. There is no cloud sync, no backup to external servers, and no way for us to access your data — because we have no servers to store it on.

If you delete the app, all your game data is deleted with it. You have complete control.

Website

Our companion website provides game information and support. We don't collect personal information through the site beyond what's inherent to standard web hosting (e.g., server logs showing IP and basic traffic data, which we don't use for tracking or profiling).

Data control & deletion

  • All your data lives on your device only.
  • Deleting the app removes all local game data.
  • You can reset your game progress anytime from within the app settings.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we'll update the effective date above. Continued use of the app after a change means you accept the updated policy.
