How It Works

Master the art of word gravity.

Letter Gravity combines strategic thinking with quick reflexes. Watch as letter blocks fall down your screen. Your goal: arrange them into valid words before they stack too high.

The higher you stack, the harder it gets. But that's the fun of it. Every game is a fresh challenge, and every word you make is a victory. Whether you're playing for a few minutes or hours, Letter Gravity keeps you engaged with pure, distraction-free gameplay.

No ads interrupting your flow. No data collection tracking your moves. Just you, the falling letters, and the satisfaction of creating words under pressure.
